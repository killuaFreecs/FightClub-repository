Task organization  
Break down complex projects into smaller, actionable items. This makes progress measurable and less overwhelming.

Prioritization  
Helps distinguish urgent bug fixes from long-term improvements, ensuring deadlines are met.

Collaboration  
Shared todo lists (e.g., GitHub Issues, Jira, Trello) keep teams aligned and accountable.

Documentation  
Inline TODO comments in code serve as reminders for future improvements or unfinished features.

Progress tracking  
Checking off tasks provides motivation and a clear sense of accomplishment.
